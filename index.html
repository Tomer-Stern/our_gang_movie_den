Where this comes from. Every entry was written by hand in a notebook, then photographed and transcribed — 893 films to start with, the earliest from 1920. The ratings are exactly as written, on the notebook’s own nought-to-five scale.

How it stays current. The log lives in a Google Sheet. Add a film there and it appears here on the next refresh; the poster, runtime and synopsis follow within a few hours, looked up automatically. Anything typed into the sheet by hand always wins over the lookup.
